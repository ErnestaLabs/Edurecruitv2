# EduRecruitment Design System

## Colors
- Primary: #1B2A4A (Deep navy — trust, authority)
- Secondary: #C9A84C (Warm gold — premium, aspirational)
- Accent: #E8D5B7 (Champagne — subtle elegance)
- Background: #FAFAF8 (Warm white/cream)
- Surface: #F5F3EF (Warm grey)
- Border: #E6E2DA (Warm light grey)
- Text: #1A1714 (Warm near-black)
- Text muted: #4A4540 (Warm dark grey)
- Success: #2D8A4E

## Typography
- Headings: Instrument Serif (serif, editorial feel)
- Body: Plus Jakarta Sans (clean, modern)
- Scale: 12/14/16/18/20/24/30/36/48/60/72

## Spacing
- Base: 8px
- Scale: 8/16/24/32/48/64/96/128
- Section padding: 6rem

## Components
- Cards: Minimal, generous padding, subtle border, no shadow stacking
- Buttons: Pill or subtle radius, solid navy or outline gold
- Navigation: Clean, transparent-to-solid on scroll
- Forms: Warm inputs, gold focus ring, clear labels

## Motion
- Scroll reveals: fade-in-up with 0.6s ease-out
- No bounce/elastic easing
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion
- Subtle hover lifts (translateY -2px)

## Anti-patterns to avoid
- Purple gradients
- Inter font (use Plus Jakarta Sans)
- Pure black/gray (always tint warm)
- Cards inside cards
- Dashboard UI aesthetics
- Generic stock photos
- Bounce animations
- Gray text on colored backgrounds
