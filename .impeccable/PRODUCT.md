# EduRecruitment.co.uk — Product Context

## What we're building
Premium education consultancy website for a founder-led UK consultancy owned by two young female consultants.

## Audience
- Primary: UK students (16-18) and parents seeking undergraduate admissions support
- Secondary: International students seeking UK placements
- Decision-makers: Parents (40-55, professional, value ROI)
- Users: Students (16-18, digital-native, value authenticity)

## Brand lane
Premium founder-led consultancy — warm, personal, trustworthy, modern.
NOT generic recruitment, NOT SaaS, NOT AI-generated.

## Voice
Warm, confident, aspirational. Like a knowledgeable older sister, not a corporate advisor.

## Anti-references
- Purple gradients
- Excessive rounded cards
- Dashboard aesthetics
- Default Inter typography everywhere
- Meaningless animations
- Bounce/elastic easing
- Gray text on colored backgrounds
- Cards nested inside cards
- Generic stock photography

## Design direction
- Editorial feel with generous whitespace
- Serif headings (Instrument Serif), clean sans body
- Warm minimalism — cream/warm tones, not pure white/gray
- Deep navy + warm gold palette
- Real founder photos, not stock
- Subtle scroll-triggered reveals, no gratuitous animation
