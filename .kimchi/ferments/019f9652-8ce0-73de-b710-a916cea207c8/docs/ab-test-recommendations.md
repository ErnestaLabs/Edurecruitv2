# A/B Test Recommendations — Education Recruitment Landing Page

## Priority 1: Hero Headline

**Test:** "It's Not Too Late. University Is Waiting." vs "Free University Guidance for Adults Returning to Education"

**Hypothesis:** The emotional headline (current) may resonate more with mature students who are anxious about age, but the benefit-driven alternative may convert better with action-oriented visitors.

**Metric:** CTA click-through rate (Book a Free Chat)

---

## Priority 2: CTA Button Copy

**Test:** "Book a Free Chat" vs "Start Your Journey" vs "Get Free Advice"

**Hypothesis:** "Free Chat" is low-commitment and friendly, but "Get Free Advice" may signal more value. "Start Your Journey" is aspirational.

**Metric:** Form submission rate

---

## Priority 3: Social Proof Position

**Test:** Testimonials before Features (current: Features → Process → Founder → Testimonials) vs Testimonials immediately after Hero

**Hypothesis:** Mature students' #1 objection is "am I too old?" — seeing peer stories earlier may reduce drop-off.

**Metric:** Scroll depth to CTA section

---

## Priority 4: Trust Badge Content

**Test:** Current badges (100% Free, Funded by Partner Universities, 500+ Placed, 4.9/5) vs badges emphasizing specific guarantees (e.g., "No Contract, No Commitment", "Free 15-Min Chat")

**Hypothesis:** Risk-reversal badges may reduce friction more than volume/rating badges.

**Metric:** Form submission rate

---

## Priority 5: Contact Form Length

**Test:** 3 fields (name, email, phone) vs 2 fields (name, email only)

**Hypothesis:** Removing phone may increase form completions, but phone allows immediate callback which is a key conversion driver for this audience.

**Metric:** Form completion rate vs quality of leads

---

## Priority 6: Founder Story Placement

**Test:** Founder story between Process and Testimonials (current) vs founder story in Hero section (inline with headline)

**Hypothesis:** Seeing real founders early builds trust for skeptical mature students, but may distract from the core value prop.

**Metric:** Scroll depth + CTA click rate
